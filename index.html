How it works

  1. 1

    Upload your RA Bill Excel workbook

    Supports .xlsx and .xls files with any number of sheets.

  2. 2

    Choose extraction mode & enter the Item Number

    Basic: civil measurements.  Steel: reinforcement bars (add Item Name / grade).

  3. 3

    Click Extract

    The engine scans every sheet, auto-detects tables, applies header merging, and strips totals.

  4. 4

    Review & download

    Search, sort, and paginate the preview table, then export to .xlsx or .csv.
